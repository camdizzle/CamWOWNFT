# CamWOW Arena — Player Guide

Welcome to CamWOW Arena, where your NFTs race, battle, and earn. This guide covers everything you need to know as a player — how to compete, earn coins, use buffs, level up your NFTs, and earn PBP token rewards in premium races.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Connect** your Twitch account and link your Solana wallet
2. **Enter** your NFT into a race lobby (up to 2 per race)
3. **Watch** your marble compete across 5 terrain zones
4. **Earn** coins from placement, quests, and achievements
5. **Spend** coins on buffs to gain an edge in future races

---

## Your NFT Stats

Every NFT has 6 core stats that determine how it performs in races and battles:

| Stat | Race Effect | Battle Effect |
|------|------------|---------------|
| **Speed** | Base movement per tick | Initiative + dodge chance |
| **Agility** | Shortcut chance through obstacles | Dodge chance |
| **Stamina** | Delays fatigue (keeps speed longer) | HP pool + late-round sustain |
| **Luck** | Random speed burst chance | Critical hit chance |
| **Toughness** | Collision damage reduction | HP pool + damage reduction |
| **Charisma** | Crowd boost chance | Intimidation (reduces enemy damage) |

Stats grow through progression as you race and battle (more on that below).

## Racing

### How Races Work

Races are scheduled **every 4 hours** (6 per day). When a lobby opens, you can enter up to **2 NFTs per race**. The race needs a minimum of **6 racers** to start (drops to 3 after extension periods). Once enough racers join, a **30-second countdown** begins.

### The Track — 5 Terrain Zones

Each race covers 5 zones. In each zone, one stat gets a major boost while others are dampened:

| Zone | Track % | Dominant Stat |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Sprint Start | 0–20% | Speed |
| Obstacle Course | 20–40% | Agility |
| Endurance Stretch | 40–60% | Stamina |
| Lucky Gauntlet | 60–80% | Luck |
| Final Stretch | 80–100% | Charisma |

A well-rounded NFT performs consistently. A specialized one can dominate its zone but may fall behind in others.

### Chaos Events

Random events fire throughout the race to keep things unpredictable:

- **Wind Gust** — pushes a racer forward
- **Rockslide** — knocks a racer back
- **Pack Shuffle** — compresses the field together
- **Position Swap** — two random racers switch places

These trigger at roughly every 10% of the race, so no lead is ever safe.

## Premium Races — Earn PBP

This is the big one. Premium races cost PBP to enter but pay out PBP to the top finishers.

### How It Works

- **Entry fee:** 50 PBP per NFT
- All entry fees go into a **prize pool** for that race
- You can still enter up to 2 NFTs (100 PBP total)

### PBP Prize Distribution

| Placement | % of Prize Pool |
|-----------|----------------|
| **1st Place** | **40%** |
| **2nd Place** | **15%** |
| **3rd Place** | **10%** |
| Season Treasury | 35% |

**Example:** 10 racers enter = 500 PBP pool
- 1st gets **200 PBP**
- 2nd gets **75 PBP**
- 3rd gets **50 PBP**
- 175 PBP goes to the **season prize pool** (distributed at end of season)

The more racers that enter, the bigger the pot. A packed 16-entry premium race has an 800 PBP pool — 1st place takes home **320 PBP**.

### Season Prize Pool

35% of every premium race feeds into the season treasury. This accumulates over the entire season and is distributed to top leaderboard finishers at season end. The longer the season runs, the bigger the final payout.

## Battles

1v1 combat between NFTs. A different way to earn XP and progress your NFT.

### How Battles Work

- Each fighter gets an HP pool based on Toughness and Stamina
- Fighters take turns attacking for up to 20 rounds
- Initiative (who goes first) is based on Speed + a dice roll
- The first NFT to drop to 0 HP loses

### What Stats Do in Battle

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Toughness** | +3 HP per point, reduces incoming damage |
| **Stamina** | +2 HP per point, sustains damage in late rounds |
| **Speed** | Higher initiative, slight dodge chance |
| **Agility** | Main dodge stat |
| **Luck** | Critical hit chance (1.8x damage) |
| **Charisma** | Intimidation chance (reduces enemy attack by 60%) |

### Battle Cooldown

Each NFT has a **4-hour cooldown** after a battle before it can fight again.

## NFT Progression — Leveling Up

Your NFT gets stronger the more you use it. Two progression systems run in parallel:

### Overall NFT Level (max 50)

Earned through races and battles:

| Activity | XP Earned |
|----------|----------|
| 1st place race | 120 XP |
| 2nd place race | 85 XP |
| 3rd place race | 60 XP |
| 4th–8th place | 30 XP |
| Battle win | 80 XP |
| Battle loss | 25 XP |

Reaching max level 50 takes roughly **121,000 XP** — about 11 months of dedicated play.

### Individual Stat Progression (max 15 levels per stat)

Stats gain XP based on what you do:

**After a race:**
- 1st place: +3 XP to your highest base stat, +1 stamina
- 2nd place: +2 XP to speed, +1 stamina
- 3rd place: +1 XP to your lowest base stat, +1 stamina
- 4th+: +1 stamina

**After a battle:**
- Win: +2 toughness, +1 luck, +1 agility
- Loss: +1 stamina, +1 charisma, +1 speed

Each stat level requires progressively more XP. Maxing a single stat to level 15 takes over 100 wins.

## Tips for New Players

1. **Start with free races** to learn how your NFT performs and build up coins
2. **Buy common buffs first** — Nitro Boost and Lucky Penny are cheap and effective
3. **Pay attention to your stats** — if your NFT has high Agility, Clone Sprint is devastating
4. **Battle regularly** — it's free XP and levels up your combat stats
5. **Complete daily quests** — small coins add up over time
6. **Enter premium races when ready** — top 3 always profits, and the season pool adds long-term upside
7. **Use 2 NFTs per race** when you can — doubles your chances and XP gain
